# Privacy Policy — Spidey Bot

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spidey Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects when you use it on Discord, and how that information is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Bot (running any command), we store the following in a private database tied to your Discord user ID:

- **Your Discord user ID** — used as the sole identifier for your in-game profile. We do not collect your Discord username, avatar, or email address.
- **In-game economy data** — wallet/bank balances, reputation level, suit integrity, daily streaks, and similar game statistics.
- **In-game inventory** — items, gadgets, and collectibles you own, buy, sell, or are given.
- **Transaction history** — a log of changes to your in-game currency, kept for balance-auditing and anti-abuse purposes.
- **Cooldown timestamps** — when you last used specific commands, to enforce cooldowns.
- **Marketplace listings** — items you list for sale to other players, visible to anyone using the Bot.
- **Ally/relationship data** — in-game "happiness" values and gift history for the Bot's ally feature.
- **Discord role and Server Boost status** — for perks exclusive to our official Discord server, we check whether you hold a qualifying role (including whether you're currently boosting that server) to determine perk eligibility. This check only ever looks at your membership in our official server — never any other server the Bot is added to — and we store only the resulting perk tier, not your full role list.
- **Patreon account link** *(only if you connect it yourself)* — if you choose to link your Patreon account (e.g. via a `/patreon link`-style command), we store your Patreon account ID, your current pledge/tier status, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens Patreon issues us for that connection. These tokens are used solely to periodically re-check your subscription tier, so perks stay accurate if you upgrade, downgrade, or cancel. We do not use them to access any other information on your Patreon account.

We do **not** collect message content, voice data, IP addresses, or any information beyond what's needed to run the game.

## 2. How We Use This Information

Collected data is used exclusively to operate the Bot's game features — tracking your progress, enforcing cooldowns, running the in-game economy and marketplace, verifying eligibility for booster/subscriber perks, and (for authorized server administrators only) diagnosing and moderating misuse.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Data is never used for advertising. Linking your Patreon account necessarily involves an exchange of data with Patreon itself (to verify your subscription) — this is not a sale or third-party sharing on our part, but Patreon's own handling of your data on their end is governed by Patreon's own privacy policy, not this one.

## 3. Data Storage

Data is stored in a database on the server hosting the Bot. It is only accessible to the Bot's operator(s) and is not shared with any external service.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as the Bot remains active in a server you're a member of, or until you request deletion. To request deletion of your data, contact the Bot's operator (see Section 6). Once deleted, your in-game profile and all associated progress cannot be recovered.

If you're a server administrator, removing the Bot from your server stops it from collecting any new data from that server going forward — it does not, by itself, delete data already stored from before removal. To have existing data deleted as well, contact the Bot's operator directly.

If you've linked your Patreon account, you can unlink it at any time (e.g. via a `/patreon unlink`-style command, once available), which deletes your stored Patreon account ID and revokes our stored access/refresh tokens. This does not cancel your actual Patreon pledge — that's managed on Patreon's own platform.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Use of the Bot is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service, which requires users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age beyond what Discord itself already collects.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your data, can be sent to: **betches.py** on Discord.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Bot's features change. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
